# Guidelines

Welcome. This is a self-directed data exercise — there's no walkthrough, no starter notebook, and no list of steps to follow.

## What to expect

- The problem statement (`PROBLEM_STATEMENT.md`) describes the data and what we want you to produce. It's intentionally light on implementation detail.
- `data/raw/` is everything you get. Inspecting it — figuring out what each file is, how it's structured, how it's encoded — is part of the exercise.
- Use whatever tools, libraries, or language you're comfortable with. Python/pandas is a safe default but not required.
- If something about a file seems unusual, that's worth investigating rather than working around.

## How we'll evaluate you

- Correctness of your final output matters, but so does how you got there.
- We're looking at your judgment: what you noticed, what assumptions you made, how you validated your own work, and whether your reasoning holds up when you explain it.
- There's no single "right" pipeline. Reasonable, well-justified choices are what we want to see — including choices we might not have made ourselves.

## Logistics

- Take the time you need.
- Submit your code/notebook, your final output, and a short write-up of your approach and findings.
- If you get stuck on something ambiguous, make a reasonable assumption, state it clearly in your write-up, and move on. Don't wait on us to unblock you.

Good luck.
